Aims & Objectives

The objectives of "Aghosh-e-Gulzar-e-Madina" are to provide shelter and good education to the most deserving orphans, ensure their safe future and to turn them into socially productive human beings. Our vision is to provide an atmosphere where all the children can grow up healthy, protected from harm and educated so that they can reach their full potential. Every day we're working to turn this vision into a reality. No matter who they are or where they are born, we will reach out to the neediest and deserving children wherever and whenever they need us. The orphans will be entertained with all the facilities i.e. food, clothing, education and shelter etc. All these services will be provided free of cost.

  • Education

 The educational activities of an institution are of superlative importance. Our aim is to furnish all the orphan children with best education. AGM welfare foundation will establish separate academic blocks for their best possible education.

AGM will ensure quality education for about 900 orphans putting forward

a)      Primary Education

b)      Secondary Education

c)      Higher Secondary Education 

d)     Vocational Education

  • Islamic Education

Our aim is to make the children good human beings and enlighten them with all basic knowledge of Islam. For this purpose students will be provided with the facilities of Hifz-ul-Quran and different disciplines of Islamic education to make them true Muslims. Apart from making the children advantageous members of society in different ways i.e. doctors, engineers, teachers etc. AGM aims at making every child a person of pious character by building inside them the roots of Islam so that they should be dignified in this world and the world hereafter.

  • Additional Training

Every child needs individual attention for the solution of his educational and psychological problems and for polishing his qualities. Capable tutors will be available to meet this need, who will have ability to solve the educational and psychological problems of the children through individual motivational attention. Apart from it, a separate library and computer lab will be there in boys’ and girls’ academic blocks for the sound character building of the children and to make them skillful in basic technology like computer respectively.

  • Recreational activities

Apart from the formal education, recreation is also essential for the mental and spiritual growth of a child. In order to ensure the physical and psychological fitness of children, different recreational activities will be a part of the project along with educational activities throughout the year. Participation of children in the games between Asar and Maghrib prayers will be ensured. The weekly sports competitions will also be held for this purpose.

Keeping in view the religious requirements of the children, their participation in various spiritual gatherings like Qirat, Nasheed, and speech competitions will be ensured so that they will able to become good Muslims through their exposure to the actual teachings of Islam.

  • Food and Cloth

The provision of healthy and balanced diet for the children will be one of the basic priorities of AGM. The menu of meals will be decided in a manner that all the children will get sufficient energy from food for their physical activities. Fruit, milk and other eating stuff like healthy meal, ice cream, juices and biscuits will be a part of their diet plan. New dresses will be made for children according to the seasonal requirements and other religious occasions.

  • Medical Facilities

Our aim also includes the establishment of a double story hospital (Trauma Center having all possible medical facilities) for children. Qualified and certified doctors will be available for complete medical treatment. Special care of children will be ensured in case of any health related complaint.

  • Other Prominent Features

High quality residential facilities for children will be available in the hostels (separate for boys and girls). Every child will have his separate bed, study table, study chair and cupboard. Apart from this a separate stationary shop, canteen, mess hall, and mosque will also be a part of this project.

  • Associations with Certified Firms, National and Multinational Companies

As explained earlier the main purpose of AGM is to make orphans productive and successful citizens of the society. Our objective is to stand up with the orphans even after the completion of their education. So by collaboration with certified firms, national and multinational companies, our organization will let them reach their possible practical level by awarding them suitable jobs.

Aghosh-e-Gulzar-e-Madina

A non-profit welfare foundation providing shelter, education and a future to orphans — Multan & Lodhran, Pakistan.
