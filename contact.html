 Remember...!!!

The Holy Prophet Hazrat Muhammad(P.B.U.H) said:

"I and the person who looks after an orphan and provides for it will be in paradise like this (indicating His index and middle fingers together)"

[Narrated Hazrat Sah'l bin Sa'd(Allah be pleased with him), Bukhari]

There was a time period (1998-2013) when Syed Shoukat Hussain Bukhari was standing alone, thinking of how he alone could initiate this grand mission to wipe the tears of many poor orphans and to bring smiles and confidence on their innocent faces. But now by the grace of God and company of many dedicated people, even construction of Aghosh-e-Gulzar-e-Madina has also been started which shows that joining hands for any great cause is always fruitful.

Main Akela Hi Chala Tha Sou'e Manzil....!!!

Log Miltay Gaey, Karwaan Banta Gaya....!!!

(I alone started the journey towards the destination, people accompanied me gradually and hence there was a caravan to travel together)

No doubt, we are dreaming and working for a long-term project, that may take many years to reach to its realization. But surely it can be completed in a few years or months if many people will join us worldwide so that by putting hands in hands together we will turn this dream into reality and very soon we will be able to see the poor orphans having smiles on their faces, by letting them study, play and have food in best possible environment of AGM.

We believe that every kind hearted person has a soft deep corner inside him for the orphans, but need was there for a platform. By the grace of God now we have it!

Come forward to become the pillars of this mission through every possible assistance in the form of moral support, dedication, services, donations or even starting another orphan care organization at your place.

When you will come through this message, don't stop and stand up to play your part towards humanity, so that after taking this project of AGM at its peak, we may start another project like this till this mission should be a mission of every human being putting behind the differences of religion, cast, country, race or area....!!!!!
