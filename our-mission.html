Our Mission

OUR MISSION IS TO HELP THOSE WHO NEED IT!CHANGE THE LIFE OF THOSE, WHO HAVE NO HOPE!

 

 

 To state about the mission I would like to quote Oseola McCarty “I can’t do everything. But I can do something to help somebody. And what I can do I will”. So to do what I can, I want to give the poor, needy and oppressed orphans an honorable and respectable place in all the fields of society by facilitating them with basic needs, education, healthy food and a comfortable shelter. I want to dedicate all my efforts to do more than something until my last breath for the entire satisfaction of my soul and to leave a valuable asset in this world so that this sacred mission may not end up with my life rather it may flourish with leaps and bounds lasting forever. I am not sure when my life will end or when am I going to take the last breath, but apart from all this, my hopes are high to leave much more than something for the humanity.

Aghosh-e-Gulzar-e-Madina

A non-profit welfare foundation providing shelter, education and a future to orphans — Multan & Lodhran, Pakistan.
