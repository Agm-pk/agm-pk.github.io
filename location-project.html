Location & Project

 Location

Link Road Sajjan Pur (2km), near Northern Bypass, Main Khanewal Road Multan.

 

 

 

Project

The area of this project is 7 Acre (304920 Sq. feet). This project is designed for about 900 orphans. It will consist of two separate triple story academic blocks for male and female students with each block capable of accommodating almost 450 students respectively. Apart from it two separate triple story hostels for boys and girls will be constructed with each hostel accommodating 450 students respectively. Each room will be well-furnished containing attached bathrooms and separate beds for 10 students. Ground floor of both academic and hostel blocks will be allotted for Administrative purposes while the rest of the floors will be available for academic activities and residence of students respectively. This project also includes a large double story Mosque for more than 500 people. A large double story hospital will be a part of this project that will have 3 separate portions, one for boys, 2nd for girls and a 3rd fully separated portion for treatment of the poor living in the surrounding areas of AGM. The project will also entail large grassy plots and play grounds for indoor and outdoor games e.g. badminton, table tennis, football, cricket etc. A separate mini colony will also be constructed for the residence of academic and administrative staff of AGM.
