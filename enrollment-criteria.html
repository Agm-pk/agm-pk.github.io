Enrollement Criteria

The primary criterion for the selection of orphans for Aghoshe-e-Gulzar-e-Madina will be that the orphan children (girls or boys) should be financially deprived and poor.The orphan children will be selected in accordance with the following preferences:

.

.

1. The poor orphans who don’t have both of their parents 2. The poor orphans whose father is no more alive 3. The poor orphans whose mother is no more alive 4. The poor children having both their parents but, their parents have poor financial status not in a position to educate their children

 Note: No discrimination of area, province or cast etc. will be entailed for the selection of children for AGM.

Special quota

Special quota will be therefor those children who have been deprived of their parents, homes and schools becauseof sudden calamities (earthquakes and floods etc.).

Aghosh-e-Gulzar-e-Madina

A non-profit welfare foundation providing shelter, education and a future to orphans — Multan & Lodhran, Pakistan.
